## First-time setup and data transfer

To complete initial device onboarding:
1. Power on the device: Press and hold the Power button on the right side for approximately 3 seconds until the Nexa logo appears.
2. Select language and region: Choose your preferred language and system locale, then accept the End User License Agreement.
3. Network connection: Connect to a secure Wi-Fi network or insert a valid nano-SIM card to activate mobile data.
4. Account setup: Sign in with your primary Google Account to enable Google Play services and cloud backup sync.
5. Data migration: When prompted with "Copy apps & data", connect your previous Android device or iPhone using the included USB-C cable or select wireless transfer via the Google Switch interface to transfer contacts, photos, messages, and applications.
6. Device security: Register a biometric fingerprint and configure a fallback 6-digit PIN, alphanumeric password, or pattern.

If the device appears unresponsive during initial setup, ensure it is connected to a reliable Wi-Fi network and has at least 30% battery charge. Do not interrupt automated system setup procedures.

## Battery, charging, and thermal management

All Nexa smartphones feature high-density 5000mAh lithium-ion battery cells equipped with hardware-level thermal safeguards.

Maximum charging input ratings by model:
- Nexa Lite: Up to 18W Fast Charging.
- Nexa One: Up to 33W Fast Charging (approx. 50% in 28 minutes).
- Nexa One Pro: Up to 67W Turbo Charge (approx. 0-100% in 42 minutes with official 67W charger).

Standard charging behavior:
Charging speeds naturally taper once the battery level surpasses 80% to protect cell chemistry and extend total battery lifespan. Ambient room temperature affects charging speed; high ambient temperatures will trigger automated thermal throttling to keep the battery within safe temperature thresholds.

Troubleshooting slow charging:
1. Verify that the USB-C cable is fully inserted into both the phone's port and the wall adapter.
2. Inspect the phone's USB-C charging port for pocket lint, dust, or physical debris using a dry, soft non-conductive brush.
3. Test charging using an alternate certified USB Type-C cable and a compatible high-wattage power adapter.
4. Verify the power adapter's rated output: an adapter rated below your model's maximum wattage will charge at a reduced speed.
5. Perform a soft restart of the device to terminate power-intensive background applications.

Critical Safety Warning — Battery Hazards:
If a device exhibits battery swelling, a bulging back panel, emitted smoke, burning odor, popping sounds, or dangerous overheating while charging:
- Immediately disconnect the power source if it is safe to do so.
- Discontinue all use of the device immediately.
- Move the device away from beds, sofas, carpets, and all flammable materials, placing it on a heat-resistant surface (such as ceramic tile or metal).
- Do not attempt to puncture, press, or open the battery or device chassis.
- Escalate the case immediately to human support.

Battery backup varies according to screen refresh rate, display brightness, 5G network signal strength, background sync, and active processing load. Fixed hours of battery runtime cannot be promised.

## Display, camera and performance issues

Display Troubleshooting:
- If experiencing touch unresponsiveness, flickering, or display stutter, restart the smartphone.
- For Nexa One Pro, check display settings under Settings > Display > Screen Refresh Rate and ensure Dynamic 120Hz is selected.
- If visual anomalies occur (lines, colored banding, dead pixels, or black spots), verify whether the anomaly appears across all apps or only within a specific application.
- Request clear photos or videos from the customer showing the defect under steady lighting.
- Note: Impact cracks, hairline glass fractures, or shattered OLED panels indicate physical shock and require inspection.

Camera Troubleshooting:
1. Gently clean the external camera lens cover using a clean, soft microfiber cloth to remove smudges or oil residue.
2. Force stop the Camera application: Go to Settings > Apps > Camera > Force Stop, then tap Storage > Clear Cache.
3. Verify that no background third-party applications (such as social media or barcode scanners) are holding exclusive access to camera hardware.
4. Navigate to Settings > System > Software Update to verify that the latest imaging firmware is installed.

Performance and System Lag:
- Restart the device to flush temporary memory allocations.
- Verify available storage space under Settings > Storage. Devices with less than 10% available storage may experience performance degradation. Advise removing unused media or clearing cached files.
- Close unused applications running in the background.
- Test the device in Safe Mode (press and hold Power, then tap and hold Power Off on screen until "Reboot to Safe Mode" appears) to determine whether third-party apps are causing instability.
- Do not instruct customers to perform a factory data reset without first confirming that all valuable data has been safely backed up to cloud or external storage. Persistent hardware problems must be escalated.

## SIM, network and connectivity

Troubleshooting cellular signal and mobile data:
1. Verify that Airplane Mode is turned off (toggle on for 10 seconds, then toggle off to reset cellular radio antennas).
2. Restart the smartphone.
3. Power off the phone, use the official SIM ejector tool to inspect the nano-SIM card for scratches or dust, and reinsert it firmly into the tray.
4. Test the nano-SIM card in another compatible unlocked smartphone to verify carrier account status and SIM validity.
5. Check with the mobile network operator to ensure the account has an active 5G/4G plan and no carrier network outages in the area.
6. Reset network settings: Go to Settings > System > Reset Options > Reset Wi-Fi, Mobile & Bluetooth.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Troubleshooting:
- Toggle Wi-Fi off and on.
- Forget the Wi-Fi network and reconnect by re-entering the network security passkey.
- Power cycle the local Wi-Fi router.
- Note: Nexa Mobile cannot resolve carrier-side outages, SIM card account locks, or unpaid carrier service suspensions. Persistent hardware connectivity faults must be escalated after troubleshooting.

3. If an update is available, download and install over a stable Wi-Fi connection.
4. Maintain at least 50% battery level or connect the phone to its charger during installation.

Important: System updates are distributed in phased regional rollout stages to ensure stability. Specific calendar release dates for future software versions cannot be promised.

## Physical damage and device faults

Handling physical trauma and non-responsive devices:
- Any smartphone exhibiting a shattered front display, cracked rear glass, bent metal frame, or liquid submersion must be evaluated by certified technicians.
- Customers must never be instructed to disassemble, unseal, or perform internal hardware repairs. Attempting self-repair voids the manufacturer warranty and creates personal injury hazards.

Troubleshooting a phone that will not turn on (black screen / no boot):
1. Connect the phone to a verified, working USB Type-C wall charger and certified cable.
2. Allow the device to charge continuously and undisturbed for at least 30 minutes (deeply depleted batteries require trickle charging before the display can power on).
3. With the charger still connected, press and hold the Power button firmly for 15 seconds to initiate a forced hardware reset.

If the device remains completely unresponsive after completing the 30-minute charge and 15-second forced reboot:
- Collect the customer's Amazon Order Number, smartphone model, purchase date, and symptom description.
- Escalate the ticket to human support for technical assessment.
