
# Shipping and orders

## Order placement

Nexa Mobile smartphones and accessories are available through our Amazon product listings.

Customers can browse available models, colours, configurations, and current pricing directly on Amazon.

All orders placed through Amazon are processed and fulfilled according to Amazon's ordering and delivery procedures.

## Delivery tracking

Customers can track their orders through:

Amazon > Your Orders > Select the Nexa Mobile order > Track Package.

The tracking page displays the latest shipment updates and estimated delivery date.

Nexa Mobile support cannot directly access, modify, or control Amazon's delivery operations.

If a parcel is delayed, marked as delivered but not received, or has an unusual tracking status, escalate the conversation to a colleague.

## Delivery addresses

Customers should verify their delivery address before completing checkout.

Address changes depend on the order's processing and shipping status.

Nexa Mobile cannot directly change an address on an Amazon order.

Customers should check whether Amazon provides an address-editing option in Your Orders.

## Missing or incorrect items

If a customer receives an incorrect product, missing accessory, or incomplete package, collect:

- Amazon order number.
- Product ordered.
- Product received.
- Description of the discrepancy.
- Photograph of the package and its contents, if available.

A colleague reviews the issue and confirms the next steps.

## Damaged deliveries

If a smartphone or accessory arrives damaged:

1. Ask the customer to retain the original packaging.
2. Request photographs of the outer packaging.
3. Request photographs of the damaged product.
4. Collect the Amazon order number.

Do not ask customers to use a visibly damaged or potentially unsafe device.

Escalate the request to a colleague for review.

## Shipping charges

Shipping charges, delivery fees, and any applicable taxes are displayed during Amazon checkout.

Nexa Mobile support cannot independently modify Amazon shipping charges.

## Service and replacement shipments

Any manufacturer-approved service shipment or replacement arrangement is handled by a colleague.

Do not promise dispatch dates, tracking numbers, or delivery timelines unless confirmed by the responsible team.

Customers receive shipment updates through the applicable communication channel once an arrangement has been confirmed.
