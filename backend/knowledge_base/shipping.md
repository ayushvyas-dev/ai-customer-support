# Shipping and orders

## Order placement

Nexa Mobile smartphones and official accessories are fulfilled through Amazon Fulfillment Centers (FBA) to ensure rapid, dependable logistics and delivery nationwide.

Placing and managing orders:
- Customers can review product specifications, real-time colorway availability, bundles, and current retail pricing on our official Amazon storefront.
- When an order is placed on Amazon, order processing, picking, packing, and dispatch are managed directly by Amazon logistics systems.
- Order confirmation emails and official purchase receipts are generated and delivered directly to the customer's registered Amazon email address.

## Delivery tracking

Amazon provides end-to-end real-time tracking for all shipped orders.

How to view tracking information:
1. Log in to your Amazon account and go to Your Orders.
2. Locate your Nexa Mobile purchase.
3. Select "Track Package" to view current transit milestones, courier carrier details, tracking numbers, and the estimated delivery date and time window.

Logistical Boundaries:
Nexa Mobile customer support does not maintain internal courier transport systems and cannot independently alter courier routes, contact dispatch drivers directly, or accelerate transit times once an order has been dispatched from an Amazon fulfillment center.

Escalation Protocol for Delivery Issues:
If a parcel is significantly delayed past the promised delivery date, displays carrier tracking exceptions (such as "Delivery Attempted - Failed" or "Package Lost in Transit"), or is marked as "Delivered" but was not physically received, escalate the conversation to a human support colleague for courier claim investigation.

## Delivery addresses

Address accuracy and dispatch rules:
- Customers must verify the accuracy of their delivery address, postal/ZIP code, and recipient telephone number prior to completing Amazon checkout.
- Once an order enters the "Preparing for Dispatch" or "Shipped" status within Amazon's automated warehouse system, address modifications are generally locked to prevent shipment routing errors.
- Nexa Mobile customer support representatives cannot directly alter, redirect, or modify shipping addresses for Amazon-fulfilled orders.

Modifying an address:
Customers should check whether the order status still permits address modification by navigating to:
Amazon > Your Orders > Order Details > Change Address.
If the option is no longer available, the customer must contact Amazon Customer Service directly for real-time delivery intercept assistance.

## Missing or incorrect items

Discrepancy Reporting:
If a customer opens their delivery and discovers an incorrect product (e.g., received a Nexa Lite instead of an ordered Nexa One Pro) or finds an item or accessory missing from the package:

1. Request the 17-digit Amazon Order Number (e.g., 112-XXXXXXX-XXXXXXX).
2. Clarify the exact item ordered versus the item physically received.
3. Obtain the barcode/LPN label information printed on the outer Amazon polybag or box if visible.
4. Request clear photographs showing the received product, the packaging box, and the shipping label barcode.

A human support colleague will cross-reference warehouse packing records and coordinate with fulfillment teams to arrange the appropriate corrective shipment or return. Support agents must never make unauthorized dispatch commitments.

## Damaged deliveries

Transit Damage Protocol:
If a smartphone package or accessory box arrives crushed, torn, unsealed, water-damaged, or punctured during courier transit:

1. Advise the customer to preserve all original outer cardboard boxes, shipping labels, and internal protective cushioning.
2. Request clear photographic evidence capturing:
   - The damaged exterior parcel showing the courier shipping label and tracking barcode.
   - The damaged inner product packaging and manufacturer seals.
   - The physical damage to the smartphone or accessory itself.
3. Collect the Amazon Order Number.
4. Safety directive: Instruct the customer not to power on, plug into a wall outlet, or charge any device that exhibits severe physical impact or deformation.

Immediately escalate the case to a human support specialist to process an expedited shipping damage claim and dispatch resolution.

## Shipping charges

Amazon handles all transaction billing and delivery calculation:
- Shipping rates, delivery fees, and expedited shipping upgrades (such as Amazon Prime One-Day or Same-Day delivery) are computed and applied by Amazon at checkout based on destination and shipping tier.
- Any applicable regional sales taxes, customs duties, or handling charges are determined by Amazon's checkout calculation.
- Nexa Mobile customer support has no administrative authority to modify Amazon delivery fees, waive shipping surcharges, or process shipping fee refunds.

## Service and replacement shipments

Shipments of authorized replacement units, service parts, or approved warranty exchanges are handled directly by our dedicated logistics and support teams.

Logistics Coordination:
- Replacement shipments are only dispatched after a human support specialist or warranty engineer has reviewed and approved the RMA case.
- Once dispatched, customers receive automated shipping confirmation containing the carrier name and live tracking link via email.
- Automated support agents must never promise exact dispatch dates, courier arrival times, or delivery estimates without formal authorization from the logistics team.
