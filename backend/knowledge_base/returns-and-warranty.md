# Returns and warranty

## Return policy

All Nexa Mobile smartphones and accessories sold through Amazon are backed by Amazon's standardized 30-day return window and customer return guidelines.

Initiating an Amazon return:
Customers can evaluate their return eligibility and initiate an authorized return directly through their Amazon customer account:
1. Log in to your Amazon account and navigate to Your Orders.
2. Locate the relevant Nexa Mobile purchase.
3. Select "Return or Replace Items".
4. Choose the applicable return reason from the dropdown menu and follow Amazon's instructions to generate a prepaid return shipping label and package return instructions.

Return conditions:
- Products must be returned in their original packaging with all included items, manuals, and accessories.
- Return policies, return windows, and refund processing timelines are determined and administered exclusively by Amazon Customer Service and Amazon Fulfillment logistics.
- Nexa Mobile customer support representatives cannot directly disburse funds, override Amazon return windows, or issue financial credits to customer payment cards.

If a customer explicitly demands an immediate refund, payment chargeback, or compensation, follow standard protocol and escalate the conversation to a human support specialist.

## Smartphone warranty coverage

All brand-new Nexa smartphones purchased through official authorized sales channels include a comprehensive 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty commencing from the verified date of delivery.

The manufacturer warranty covers hardware and manufacturing defects resulting from faulty materials or assembly workmanship under normal consumer usage:
- Internal logic boards, processors, and soldered circuit architecture.
- Display panels and touchscreen digitizer hardware (excluding physical fractures, impacts, or pressure damage; subject to technician bench verification).
- Camera sensor modules, optical image stabilization mechanisms, and flash units.
- Integrated microphones, earpieces, and loud speakers.
- Built-in USB Type-C charging port connectors and charging circuitry.
- Internal lithium-ion battery cells exhibiting premature hardware failure or manufacturing defects.

Warranty validation requirements:
Warranty service claims require a valid proof of purchase (such as the official Amazon VAT/GST sales invoice or 17-digit Amazon Order ID). Warranty coverage applies only to devices distributed through authorized sales channels and is non-transferable beyond the original purchaser.

## Warranty exclusions

The Nexa Limited Manufacturer Warranty does not apply to:
- Accidental drops, blunt impact, or mechanical stress resulting in cracked, shattered, chipped, or fractured glass, AMOLED panels, or chassis frames.
- Liquid intrusion, moisture corrosion, water immersion, or exposure to excessive humidity (evidenced by internal Liquid Contact Indicators).
- Damage resulting from unauthorized third-party repairs, teardowns, alterations, or non-certified service center intervention.
- Damage caused by using uncertified, non-compliant, or defective charging adapters, counterfeit cables, or improper electrical voltages.
- Software modifications including bootloader unlocking, custom ROM flashing, unauthorized root access, or malware infections.
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear, including minor surface scuffs, scratches, finish fading, and denting acquired through routine daily usage.
- Damage resulting from acts of nature, fire, earthquake, or extreme environmental exposure.

All warranty claims undergo comprehensive technical inspection by certified Nexa diagnostic engineers upon arrival at the authorized service center. Support agents must never confirm that an issue is definitively covered or denied prior to physical diagnostic bench evaluation.

## Battery warranty

Lithium-ion battery performance degrades gradually over time as a natural consequence of chemical aging and accumulated charge cycles.

Warranty terms for battery performance:
- The 1-Year Limited Warranty covers genuine manufacturing defects in battery cells, such as rapid sudden capacity drops (>20% drop within minutes) or internal power controller failure occurring within the warranty period.
- Standard, progressive reductions in maximum runtime resulting from normal charging cycles and usage habits are characteristic of all lithium-ion batteries and do not constitute a manufacturing defect.

Critical Battery Safety Escalation:
If a customer reports a swollen or distended battery, a bulging rear panel, smoke emission, sparks, popping sounds, or a distinct burning odor while charging or operating the device:
- Immediately advise the customer to stop using and charging the device.
- Instruct them to safely unplug the phone if safe to do so and isolate it on a non-combustible surface away from flammable materials.
- Immediately escalate the ticket with emergency priority to human support.

## How to request warranty service

To submit a formal manufacturer warranty claim for technician evaluation, customers must provide:

1. Valid Amazon Order ID (17 digits, e.g., 112-XXXXXXX-XXXXXXX) or copy of the Amazon purchase invoice.
2. Exact smartphone model and colorway.
3. Verified purchase and delivery dates.
4. Comprehensive description of the hardware fault and specific conditions under which the symptom occurs.
5. First-line troubleshooting steps already completed.
6. Clear, high-resolution photographs or video footage displaying the physical condition of the device (front screen, back cover, sides, and the fault itself).

Next Steps in Warranty Service:
- A technical support specialist will review the documentation and issue an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number with official service instructions.
- Support agents must never guarantee repair turnaround times, promise free product replacements, or confirm warranty claim approvals prior to authorized service center inspection.

## Warranty on accessories

Official Nexa charging accessories and cables:
- Nexa 33W Power Adapters, Nexa 67W Turbo Adapters, and Nexa Braided USB-C Cables carry a 6-Month Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering electrical and component defects from the date of purchase.

Cases and Screen Protectors:
- Nexa Clear Cases, Nexa Armor Cases, and Nexa Tempered Glass Screen Protectors are covered strictly for verified manufacturing flaws, packaging defects, or sizing discrepancies reported within 14 days of delivery.
- Accidental drops, cracking due to impact, scratches, yellowing from prolonged UV/chemical exposure, and standard cosmetic wear are excluded from warranty coverage.

## Products purchased outside official channels

Nexa Mobile cannot verify the authenticity, operational safety, or history of devices purchased through unauthorized third-party marketplaces, unauthorized auctions, or second-hand private sellers.

- Devices purchased through unauthorized vendors do not qualify for official Nexa manufacturer warranty coverage or direct RMA servicing.
- Customers must provide official Amazon order confirmation verifying that the transaction was completed through Nexa's authorized Amazon storefront.
- When an order's authenticity is unverified, collect available purchase records and escalate to a human specialist for account verification.
