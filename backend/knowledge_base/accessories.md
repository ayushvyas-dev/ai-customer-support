## Charger compatibility

Nexa smartphones utilize universal USB Type-C interface architecture conforming to global USB-IF Power Delivery standards.

Certified charging speeds and protocols:
- Nexa Lite supports up to 18W Fast Charging (5V/3A, 9V/2A).
- Nexa One supports up to 33W Fast Charging (11V/3A PPS, 9V/3A).
- Nexa One Pro supports up to 67W Turbo Charge (11V/6.1A PPS, 20V/3.25A).

Compatibility guidelines:
- Safe Interoperability: Connecting a higher-wattage certified charger (such as the Nexa 67W adapter) to a Nexa Lite or Nexa One is completely safe. The smartphone's internal power management IC automatically negotiates the exact voltage and current required and will not exceed the phone's maximum rated input.
- Slower Charging on Lower-Wattage Chargers: Using standard 5W or 10W legacy USB chargers or computer USB ports will result in substantially slower charging times.
- Cable Rating: To achieve full 67W Turbo Charge speeds on the Nexa One Pro, both the power adapter and the USB-C cable must be rated for high-wattage power delivery.
- Prohibition of Uncertified Hardware: Avoid using unbranded, counterfeit, non-certified, or damaged adapters and cables, as they lack certified over-voltage, over-current, and thermal safeguards.

## Case and screen protector compatibility

Protective cases, armor covers, and tempered glass protectors are strictly model-specific and are not universally interchangeable across different phone models.

Fitment rules:
- Nexa One Pro cases will NOT fit the standard Nexa One. Even though the industrial design language is similar, the Nexa One Pro features a larger 6.7-inch chassis, distinct exterior dimensions, and a larger camera housing compared to the 6.5-inch Nexa One.
- Nexa One cases will NOT fit the Nexa Lite due to differences in chassis thickness, button placements, and rear camera array dimensions.
- Tempered glass screen protectors are cut to exact front display millimeter tolerances, speaker ear-piece cutouts, and edge curvatures for each respective model.

Customers must verify their exact device model before purchasing accessories by navigating to:
Settings > About Phone > Model Name.

If a customer receives an incorrectly sized accessory from Amazon, collect their Amazon order number, the accessory purchased, and their smartphone model to determine return or replacement options.

## Accessory troubleshooting

If an official Nexa accessory fails to function properly:

1. Visual inspection: Inspect the USB-C plug, prongs, cable braiding, and device ports for bent pins, physical cracking, warping, or contamination.
2. Port cleaning: Ensure the smartphone's USB-C port and the accessory plug are free of lint and dust. Use a soft, dry brush if necessary.
3. Cross-testing: Connect the power adapter or cable to a different known-working device and wall outlet to isolate whether the failure is caused by the accessory or the phone.
4. Soft reboot: Restart the smartphone to reset internal peripheral detection drivers if a connected accessory is not recognized.

Safety restriction: If a charging adapter or cable is visibly frayed, cracked, produces sparking, or emits excessive heat or an electrical odor, immediately discontinue use. Do not attempt repairs.

If the accessory remains non-functional after completing basic troubleshooting, collect the customer's Amazon order number and a description of the failure for warranty evaluation.

- Collect the exact accessory name and SKU.
