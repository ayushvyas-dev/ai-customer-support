
# Returns and warranty

## Return policy

Nexa Mobile purchases made through Amazon follow Amazon's return and refund policies.

Customers can check whether their order is eligible for a return by visiting:

Amazon > Your Orders > Select the Nexa product > Return or Replace Items.

Return eligibility, available options, and deadlines depend on the order and Amazon's policies.

Nexa Mobile support cannot directly initiate, approve, or accelerate an Amazon refund.

For return eligibility questions, direct customers to their Amazon order page.

If a customer specifically requests a refund, chargeback, or money back, escalate the conversation to a colleague.

## Smartphone warranty coverage

All Nexa smartphones come with a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in:
- Internal hardware components.
- Display components, subject to inspection.
- Motherboard and internal circuitry.
- Charging port.
- Manufacturing-related battery defects.

Warranty claims require valid proof of purchase.

The warranty is applicable only to eligible devices purchased through authorized sales channels.

## Warranty exclusions

The limited warranty does not cover:
- Accidental drops and impact damage.
- Cracked or broken screens caused by physical impact.
- Liquid damage.
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Damage caused by incompatible chargers or accessories.
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear.
- Software issues caused by unauthorized modifications.

All warranty claims are subject to technical inspection and verification.

Never tell a customer that a particular issue is definitely covered or excluded without an inspection.

## Battery warranty

Battery performance naturally changes with usage and charging cycles.

The warranty covers manufacturing-related battery defects during the applicable warranty period.

Normal battery capacity reduction over time is not automatically considered a manufacturing defect.

If a customer reports battery swelling, overheating, smoke, or a burning smell, advise them to stop using and charging the device and escalate immediately.

## How to request warranty service

To request warranty assistance, customers should provide:

1. Order number or proof of purchase.
2. Smartphone model.
3. Purchase date.
4. Description of the problem.
5. Photographs or videos where relevant.

A colleague reviews the request and explains the next steps.

Do not promise a repair, replacement, or turnaround time before the device has been assessed.

## Warranty on accessories

Nexa-branded charging adapters and USB-C cables carry a 6-month limited manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.

Cases and tempered glass are covered only for verified manufacturing defects, subject to the applicable product listing and purchase terms.

Accidental damage and normal wear are not covered.

## Products purchased outside official channels

Warranty eligibility for devices purchased from unauthorized sellers cannot be guaranteed.

Ask the customer for their order details and proof of purchase.

A colleague will verify the device's eligibility before confirming warranty support.
